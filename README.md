# Tractive-System-and-Telemetry-Analysis
Formula Student technical exercise featuring a C++ tractive-system state-machine simulation and MATLAB analysis of vehicle telemetry, suspension behaviour and energy consumption.
MATLAB Telemetry Analysis

Analysed GPS and wheel speeds, throttle and brake signals, suspension travel, and battery power.

The work examines candidate wheel-spin intervals, suspension behaviour under acceleration and braking, and energy consumption and regenerative braking. A repeated plateau in the front-right suspension signal was identified for further investigation.
